KEYCLICK TRACKER — GLASSMORPHISM LOGO CONCEPTS

Five separate 1254 × 1254 PNG logo concepts.

01 — KC Frosted Monogram
Translucent glass KC letters, a mouse tucked into the C, and a compact keyboard on a pale blue-white glass tile. Light, refined and calm.

02 — KC Glass Wordmark
A larger frosted KC monogram with keyboard and mouse, orbiting glass ribbon and the KeyClick Tracker wordmark on a deep blue glass tile. Best suited to a branded splash/marketing lockup.

03 — KC Mouse Keycap
The C encloses a simplified mouse; a keyboard sits beneath a translucent K/C mark. Darker blue glass tile with restrained cyan edge lighting.

04 — KC Light Glass
A light frosted-glass tile with white translucent KC lettering, mouse integrated in the C, and keyboard beneath. Soft powder-blue depth.

05 — KC Soft Glass Orbit
A layered rounded glass tile with a simple KC monogram, keyboard and mouse, and a single curved ribbon for movement. Cool blue-gray glass backdrop.

REUSABLE ART-DIRECTION PROMPT
Create a premium glassmorphism desktop-app logo for “KeyClick Tracker”. Use a simple, legible KC monogram as the primary mark, integrating a minimal mouse silhouette into the C and a compact keyboard motif beneath or beside the monogram. Build the icon from layered translucent frosted-glass shapes with realistic but restrained refraction, soft internal highlights, subtle bevels, gentle shadows, and a muted icy-blue / periwinkle / lavender palette over a deep blue-gray or pale frosted background. Keep the geometry clear and balanced with generous negative space. Sophisticated, polished, human-directed brand design. No cyberpunk styling, no neon tubes, no busy circuitry, no excessive glow, no extra symbols, no unnecessary text.

Note: These are raster PNG concept images, not editable SVG vector masters.
